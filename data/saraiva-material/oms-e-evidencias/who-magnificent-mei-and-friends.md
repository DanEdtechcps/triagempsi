13- year old Mei and her friends Yasmin, Addo and
Pablo are navigating the ups and downs of their first
year as official “teens”.

Nicknamed “Magnificent Mei” by her grandmother,
Mei doesn’t always feel magnificent these days.
Living in a world with many twists and turns,
Mei and her friends must learn how to deal with
their feelings, navigate relationships, overcome
challenges, and make good choices.

Join Mei on a journey where she discovers
her own abilities and realizes just how magnificent
she really is!
